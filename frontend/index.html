MarketEngine

MarketEngine is an AI-powered marketing automation platform that helps businesses and marketing teams create, schedule, and publish content across their social media accounts — including Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and YouTube — all from a single dashboard.

MarketEngine uses AI to generate posts, captions, and images, recommends the best times to publish, and provides real-time analytics so you can track engagement and reach across every connected platform. Teams and agencies can manage multiple brands with role-based access and approval workflows.
